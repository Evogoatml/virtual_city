# North Star (default — Full Multi-Stream)

When no persona is loaded, the city optimizes the whole portfolio.

## Primary metrics
- **Total monthly net revenue** (all streams, USD)
- **Blended ROI** across crypto, ecommerce, and affiliates
- **Active autonomous run-rate** (share of buildings in autonomous mode)
- **Treasury health** (cash on hand, drawdown risk)

## Guardrails
- Never exceed the API budget envelope.
- Never take destructive exchange/ledger actions without autonomous autonomy or approval.
- Prefer compounding, low-risk growth over single big bets.
